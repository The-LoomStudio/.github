# Loom Studio

**Weave worlds, interweave stories.**

A local-first creative workspace for AI roleplay and long-form interactive storytelling.

[Get started](https://github.com/The-LoomStudio/LoomStudio/blob/main/docs/guide/beta-release.md) · [Source code](https://github.com/The-LoomStudio/LoomStudio) · [Documentation](https://github.com/The-LoomStudio/LoomStudio/tree/main/docs) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/The-LoomStudio/LoomStudio/issues)

</div>

## Give your stories a world of their own

Start with a character or an idea for a setting, and weave each choice into a story you can keep exploring.

- **Explore different paths**: Develop your characters and worlds through timelines and branches.
- **Organize your creative material**: Bring characters, worldbuilding, and prompt composition together in one workspace.
- **Look behind the scenes**: Keep story prose and Agent work logs in their own spaces, with tools and extensions to enrich your creative process.

## Our projects

Loom Studio is currently in Beta. Extensions are maintained independently; see their repositories for usage and release status. Most documentation is currently in Chinese.
